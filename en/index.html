The Psalms sung: Christian worship music to pray, praise and rest in God

Amen Hallelujah Official brings you the Psalms of the Bible sung in English, one by one and in order. It is Christian praise and worship music made for your time with God: to pray in the morning, meditate on Scripture during the day and fall asleep in peace at night.

Listen to the Psalm 91 song, the psalm of protection prayed by families around the world; Psalm 23, “The Lord is my shepherd”, for peace and trust; or Psalm 100, the psalm of thanksgiving. If you are looking for psalms for sleep, calming Bible songs for anxiety or a worship song to start your day in faith, here are the Psalms as scripture songs.

Each psalm is sung to a simple, modern melody that is easy to remember, so memorizing Scripture feels as natural as singing. Listen free on YouTube, Spotify, Apple Music and Amazon Music, or ask Alexa to play your favorite Psalm.
