Privacy

At Amen Hallelujah Official we look after your data the way we would like ours to be looked after. We ask for the minimum, and we never sell or share it.

Who keeps your data

Laura, who runs the Amen Hallelujah Official project. Contact: amenhallelujahofficial@gmail.com

What data we collect and why

  • Your email and your challenge answers (theme, time, platform and time of day), only if you enter them, to send you your Psalms for each day of the 28-Day Challenge.
  • Your challenge progress (your answers, the days you have done and your gratitude notes) is stored only on your own phone or browser. It never reaches us.
  • Visit statistics: we count how many people visit each page with GoatCounter, without cookies and without any data that identifies you.

Who we work with

Your sign-up (email, theme, time option and platform) is stored in a private Google form and spreadsheet in our account, and the emails are sent from our Gmail. We don't share it with anyone.

How long

We keep your email for as long as you want to receive our emails. When you unsubscribe, we stop writing to you.

Your rights

You can unsubscribe at any time with the link at the bottom of every email (Unsubscribe page). You can also ask us to see, correct or delete your data by writing to amenhallelujahofficial@gmail.com.
